FinTech Project

PapiPay

Mobile payment technology designed for gas stations and convenience stores.

Fueling from the phone.

PapiPay was designed to let customers use an Apple or Android mobile device to activate a fuel pump and complete payment. The project combined payment flows, retail systems and a consumer mobile experience.

Yo Eleven Ventures contributed product concept, design and development leadership, coordinating implementation with an offshore development team.

Concept • Product Design • Mobile Experience • Development Leadership • FinTech
