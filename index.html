Principles

A small number of ideas guide everything we build.

Solve real problems.
Technology is valuable when it makes something meaningfully easier, faster, safer, or clearer.
Simplicity wins.
The best technology hides complexity rather than asking the user to manage it.
Trust is a feature.
Products should be dependable, transparent, privacy-conscious, and worthy of becoming part of someone's everyday life.
Build for utility.
We value lasting usefulness over attention, engagement, or novelty for its own sake.
